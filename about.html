About me

Adriano
Lezama

I'm a sports photographer based in Portugal, passionate about capturing the raw intensity and emotion of sport — from the explosive energy of futsal to the beauty of the game in motion.

Every frame is a story. My work focuses on the split-second moments that define matches, the joy of victory, and the grit behind every athlete's performance.

Available for match coverage, editorial shoots, club partnerships, and image licensing.
